# VERCUNO — Design System

Version: 1.0
Direction: Bold Editorial Minimalist
Reference: https://melon.studio/

The reference is inspiration only.
Do not copy its code, branding, or exact layout.

## 1. Design Philosophy

VERCUNO must feel like a premium international
creative technology studio.

Characteristics:

- Bold editorial typography
- Dramatic but controlled composition
- Asymmetrical layouts
- Generous negative space
- Meaningful interactions
- High visual polish
- Confident art direction

Avoid generic SaaS landing page patterns.

## 2. Brand Colors

Midnight Black: #02070A
Electric Blue: #0297DE
Cantaloupe Orange: #FC734D
Ivory White: #F5F8FF
Secondary Surface: #111820

Suggested visual distribution:

70% Midnight Black
20% Electric Blue
10% Cantaloupe Orange

White text and neutral surfaces are functional
support colors and are not part of this ratio.

Use orange sparingly for meaningful emphasis.

## 3. Typography

Headline:
Manrope ExtraBold (800)

Editorial accent:
Instrument Serif Italic

Body:
Inter Regular (400)

UI labels:
Inter Medium (500)

Desktop Hero:
112–160px, responsive to viewport.

Mobile Hero:
52–72px, adjusted to available width.

Headline line-height:
0.9–1.0

Body line-height:
1.5–1.65

Use fluid typography where appropriate.
No clipped or overflowing text.

## 4. Layout

Desktop reference:
1440 x 900

Mobile reference:
390 x 844

Additional testing:
768 x 1024
320 x 568

Desktop horizontal gutter:
48–72px

Mobile horizontal gutter:
20–24px

Use a flexible grid.
Prefer intentional asymmetry over perfect symmetry.

## 5. Component Styling

Buttons:
- Refined rounded or pill shape
- Clear interactive feedback
- Electric Blue primary action
- High-contrast label
- Visible keyboard focus

Navigation:
- Minimal
- Lightweight
- Strong alignment
- Accessible mobile menu

Cards:
- Use only when content requires them
- Avoid generic glassmorphism

Borders:
- Thin and understated
- Prefer subtle neutral colors

## 6. Visual Language

Primary visual:
Oversized editorial typography.

Secondary visual:
Monumental geometric V.

Supporting details:
- Thin orbital arcs
- Controlled orange accents
- Subtle lines
- Geometric framing

The V should feel architectural, not like
a generic stock illustration.

Preserve the established VERCUNO logo.

## 7. Motion Principles

Motion must enhance storytelling and interaction.

Preferred techniques:
- Text masking and reveal
- Staggered entrance animations
- Refined easing
- Subtle parallax
- Smooth CTA hover transitions
- Project preview interactions

Avoid:
- Scroll hijacking
- Excessive bounce
- Unnecessary 3D effects
- Constant background movement
- Distracting cursor effects

Respect prefers-reduced-motion.

Animations must not hide content permanently.

## 8. Responsive Behavior

Desktop:
- Oversized typography
- Asymmetrical editorial composition
- Decorative V on the right

Tablet:
- Scale typography and geometry
- Avoid overlaps

Mobile:
- Single-column composition
- Stacked headline
- Full-width primary CTA
- Simplified navigation
- Reduced animation complexity

## 9. Accessibility

- Semantic HTML
- WCAG AA contrast targets
- Visible focus states
- Keyboard-friendly controls
- Accessible labels
- Reduced-motion support
- No important information conveyed by color alone

## 10. Brand Mascot

Vercy is the official supporting mascot.

Do not place Vercy in the initial Hero.

Use Vercy selectively in later sections
where it improves the experience.

Avoid loading large mascot videos unnecessarily.

## 12. Quality Standard

The website should look intentional,
distinctive, fast, and professionally engineered.

Do not replace approved visual decisions
with default templates or generic patterns.
